HOW TO ADD A PHOTO TO THE STURDIHOME WEBSITE
(No coding experience needed. You'll be using an AI assistant to make the
actual changes - things like Claude Code, or a similar AI tool that can see
and edit the project files. You just need to know how to find a file on
your computer and type sentences.)


STEP 1 - Get your photo ready

Save the photo somewhere on your computer where you can find it again, like
your Desktop or Downloads folder. JPG or PNG files both work fine. If the
photo is huge (some phone photos are 5-10 MB), don't worry about shrinking
it yourself - the assistant can do that for you.


STEP 2 - Put the photo into the project folder

Open the SturdiHome project folder on your computer. Look for a folder
inside it called "public" - open that one up.

Drag your photo file into the "public" folder (or into the "public/images"
folder if there is one). That's it. You don't need to rename it, resize it,
or do anything else. Just get it into that folder.


STEP 3 - Tell your AI assistant what you did

Open a chat with your AI assistant (the same kind of tool used to build
this site) and just tell it, in plain English, what you did and what you
want. You don't need any special wording. For example, you could say:

    "I put a new photo in the public folder called family-photo.jpg.
    Can you add it to the About section of the homepage?"

or simply:

    "I added a photo to the public folder. Please use it as the new
    picture on the homepage."

The assistant will find the file, move it wherever it needs to go, and
write whatever code is needed to show it on the page. You do not need to
know the exact file name or folder - just describe what you did and what
you want, and the assistant will figure out the details and ask you if it
needs anything else.


STEP 4 - Check that it looks right

Ask the assistant to show you the result. For example:

    "Can you show me what that looks like before we publish it?"

The assistant can start a local preview, or just describe/screenshot the
change for you to review.


STEP 5 - Publish it

Once it looks good, ask the assistant to save and publish the change:

    "That looks good. Please save this and push it live."

Publishing (pushing to GitHub) automatically updates the live website
within a minute or two, at https://sturdihome.vercel.app

If nothing seems to happen after you ask, just ask directly - "did you
push this?" - some assistants pause and wait for you to confirm before
publishing.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

- Photo doesn't show up at all:
  Tell the assistant "the photo isn't showing up" - it can double check
  the file made it into the right place.

- Photo looks stretched, cropped oddly, or too big/small:
  Tell the assistant exactly what looks off, e.g. "the photo looks
  stretched" or "can you make that photo smaller."

- Page feels slow after adding the photo:
  Tell the assistant the photo feels slow to load and ask it to compress
  or resize it.

- You don't see the change on the live site yet:
  Ask the assistant to confirm the change was actually published, then
  give it a minute or two - it takes a little time to go live after
  publishing.


THAT'S THE WHOLE PROCESS

1. Save the photo somewhere on your computer.
2. Drag it into the project's "public" folder.
3. Tell the AI assistant what you did and what you want.
4. Look at the result.
5. Ask the assistant to publish it.
